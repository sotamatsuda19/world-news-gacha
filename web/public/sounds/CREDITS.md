# Sound credits

All paper sounds come from Freesound and are released under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no attribution is required,
but we record where each one came from.
They were trimmed, made mono and level-adjusted by `scripts/make-sounds.sh`.
The spin and landing sounds are not files: they are synthesized in `src/lib/sound.ts`.
